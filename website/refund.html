POLICY

Cancellation& refunds.

Plain terms for SMARAN.AI Pro, the monthly subscription. Last updated 24 September 2026.

01 · WHAT YOU BUY

SMARAN.AI Pro, billed monthly

Pro is a digital subscription for the SMARAN.AI software, charged at ₹700 per month in Indian rupees through Razorpay. Nothing physical is shipped: after payment you receive a licence key on screen straight away, and Razorpay emails your receipt. Customers outside India pay by card in INR, and their bank converts the amount.

02 · CANCELLING

Cancel any time

You can cancel whenever you like, through the link in your Razorpay subscription email or by writing to us through the feedback form. Once cancelled, you are not charged again, and Pro keeps working until the end of the month you have already paid for.

03 · REFUNDS

When we refund

If you are charged by mistake, charged twice, or charged after cancelling, we refund that charge in full. For your first Pro payment, if SMARAN.AI does not work for you, ask within 7 days and we refund it. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

04 · FREE PLAN

Personal stays free

The Personal plan costs nothing and needs no payment details. During the launch period, Pro is free too; no card is asked for, and nothing is charged unless you choose to subscribe after paid plans begin.

05 · CONTACT

Questions or a refund request

Use the feedback form and include the payment ID from your Razorpay receipt (it starts with pay_). We reply to every billing request.
